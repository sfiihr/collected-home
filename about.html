About My Collected Home

I am not an interior designer. I am a mom, a wife, and a professional who has grown to realize that I feel most at home in a space that doesn't look like everyone else's.

Over the last ten years, I've learned from trial and error, and have evolved my personal design style from "I want to look like the houses in the model homes" to a more personalized and authentic approach. This site is a reflection of that evoluation and the work that is to come. I hope you'll join me as I explore my style, find ways to integrate personal touches that reflect the history of our family, and maybe make a few more mistakes along the way. It won't always be perfect, but I promise it'll be fun.

My Design Philosophy

I believe the most interesting homes are the ones that we live in - the memories shared, the pieces collected, and the little bits of history that we collect over time. They mix old and new and are never truly finished. They lean into color and texture, feature vintage elements and objects that are inherited from friends and loved ones over years, have art with a story attached, and have just the right amount of "weird" create a style that's impossible to replicate. My goal isn't to create the perfect home. It's to create a home that is unmistakeably ours.
