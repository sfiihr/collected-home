# My Collected Home

## Project Overview

*My Collected Home* is a two-page personal interior design website documenting the evolution of my home and my personal design style.  This project explores my shift from creating coordinated, "finished" spaces to a more collected design aesthetic that incorporates personal history, meaningful objects, color, and a little bit of "weird" to keep the space uniquely ours.

This website follows the evolution of our home, from builder-grade, through early design choices, to the mindset that is shaping the next chapter of our space.

## Website

[My Collected Home](https://sfiihr.github.io/collected-home/)

### Website Pages

- Home (index.html) - Documents the evolution of my design style through written content, photos of my actual home, and my future design concept.
- About (about.html) - Provides a brief background on me, my design philosophy and inspiration, and my approach to creating a collected home.
